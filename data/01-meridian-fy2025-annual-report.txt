To our shareholders,

Fiscal 2025 was the year Meridian Freightworks stopped being a trucking company
that sells software and became a logistics platform that also moves freight.

SECTION 2. COMPANY OVERVIEW
================================================================================

2.1 Business Description

Meridian Freightworks, Inc. ("Meridian" or "the Company") is a North American
freight and logistics provider headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. The Company was
founded in 1987 as Meridian Trucking Co. and adopted its current name in 2011.
Meridian's common stock trades on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the
ticker symbol MFWX.

Meridian operates through three reportable segments:

  - Ground Freight: less-than-truckload and full-truckload transportation
    across the continental United States, Canada, and northern Mexico.
  - Cold Chain: temperature-controlled transportation and warehousing for
    food, agricultural, and pharmaceutical customers.
  - Digital Platform: MeridianOne, a subscription software product that gives
    shippers rate benchmarking, load tendering, and shipment visibility.

2.2 Fiscal Year

Meridian's fiscal year ends on December 31. References to "fiscal 2025" or
"FY2025" mean the twelve months ended December 31, 2025.

2.3 Physical Footprint

As of December 31, 2025, Meridian operated 43 distribution centers and 12
dedicated cold-storage facilities. The Company's fleet consisted of 3,140
tractors and 8,265 trailers. Of the tractor fleet, 128 units were battery
electric, up from 41 units at the end of fiscal 2024.

SECTION 4. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
================================================================================

4.1 Ground Freight

Ground Freight generated revenue of $1,842.6 million in fiscal 2025, an increase
of 7.5 percent from $1,714.2 million in fiscal 2024. Segment operating income
was $147.4 million, representing an operating margin of 8.0 percent.

Volume growth was modest at 2.1 percent measured in shipments, with the balance
of revenue growth coming from a 4.9 percent increase in revenue per shipment.
The segment exited approximately $40 million of brokered volume during the third
quarter. On-time delivery for the segment reached 94.6 percent, compared with
92.8 percent in fiscal 2024.

Ground Freight employed 7,236 people as of December 31, 2025.

4.2 Cold Chain

Cold Chain generated revenue of $734.1 million in fiscal 2025, an increase of
19.8 percent from $612.8 million in fiscal 2024. Segment operating income was
$95.4 million, representing an operating margin of 13.0 percent — the segment's
first full year above ten percent.

Growth was driven primarily by pharmaceutical customers, which grew 31 percent
year over year and now represent 22 percent of segment revenue. Temperature
compliance, measured as the share of shipments held continuously within the
contracted temperature band, was 99.3 percent.

Cold Chain employed 2,614 people as of December 31, 2025. The segment is led by
Marcus Reyes, Senior Vice President, Cold Chain.

4.3 Digital Platform

Digital Platform generated revenue of $256.3 million in fiscal 2025, an increase
of 43.7 percent from $178.4 million in fiscal 2024. Segment operating income was
$61.5 million, representing an operating margin of 24.0 percent.

Annual recurring revenue reached $271.4 million exiting the year. MeridianOne
served 1,847 active shipper accounts, up from 1,302 a year earlier. Net revenue
retention was 118 percent. The segment employed 986 people as of December 31,
2025.

4.4 Corporate and Unallocated

Corporate and unallocated costs were $52.8 million in fiscal 2025. These costs
include executive compensation, corporate legal, and shared technology services
not attributed to a reportable segment. Corporate functions employed 646 people
as of December 31, 2025.

SECTION 5. CUSTOMERS AND CONCENTRATION
================================================================================

Meridian's ten largest customers accounted for 31.4 percent of consolidated
revenue in fiscal 2025, compared with 34.9 percent in fiscal 2024. The single
largest customer, a national grocery chain served primarily by the Cold Chain
segment, accounted for 8.7 percent of consolidated revenue.

No other customer accounted for more than 5 percent of consolidated revenue in
either fiscal year. The Company's customer contracts are generally terminable on
60 to 90 days notice.

SECTION 9. RISK FACTORS
================================================================================

9.1 Operating and Market Risks

  - Driver availability. A sustained shortage of qualified commercial drivers
    could constrain capacity and increase wage costs.
  - Fuel price volatility. Approximately 68 percent of fuel cost exposure is
    recovered through customer surcharges; the remainder is unhedged.
  - Freight cycle exposure. Roughly 27 percent of Ground Freight revenue is
    priced on the spot market and reprices within 30 days.
  - Customer concentration. See Section 5.
  - Cybersecurity. A breach of the MeridianOne platform could expose customer
    shipment and pricing data.

9.2 Legal Proceedings

In August 2024, a putative class action was filed in California Superior Court
alleging that the Company misclassified approximately 1,400 owner-operator
drivers as independent contractors. The Company recorded an accrual of $18.5
million in fiscal 2025 related to this matter. The Company believes the range of
reasonably possible loss in excess of the accrual is not material.
